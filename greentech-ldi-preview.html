Welcome to GreenTech LDI

Turning climate responsibility into pathways to work, enterprise and stronger communities.

GreenTech Livelihoods Development Initiative is a faith-inspired, inclusive and professionally governed nonprofit designed to make green development locally useful, economically empowering and socially inclusive.

We work where environmental responsibility meets everyday livelihood reality—helping underserved people gain market-relevant capabilities, adopt productive technologies, build viable enterprises and participate in community-led environmental action.

Livelihood-first climate actionEnvironmental value connected to work, income, productivity or resilience.
Training-to-outcome pathwaysSkills linked to practice, tools, mentorship, jobs, enterprise and markets.
Appropriate technologyAffordable, maintainable and productive solutions for real communities.

Programmes & Initiatives

Six pillars. One connected pathway to dignified green livelihoods.

Our strategy links skills, production, technology, enterprise and stewardship instead of treating them as isolated interventions.

01

Green Skills & Decent Work

Technical skills, apprenticeships, employability and progression into decent work or enterprise.

02

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Resilient production, value addition, soil and water management, mechanisation and rural enterprise.

03

Clean Energy & Productive Technology

Solar, clean cooking, cold storage, irrigation, processing and digital tools that improve livelihoods.

04

Circular Economy & Waste-to-Value

Repair, collection, sorting, recycling, composting and viable local resource-recovery models.

05

Green Enterprise & Inclusive Finance

Business development, cooperatives, mentoring, finance readiness, productive assets and market access.

06

Stewardship & Community Resilience

Environmental education, restoration, water conservation, community greening and locally owned resilience.

Recommended first flagship

GreenTech Skills-to-Livelihoods Accelerator

A complete progression pathway—not a stand-alone training event. The proposed Makurdi founding cohort will combine technical learning with enterprise preparation, apprenticeship or placement, productive support and continued outcome tracking.

✓Approximately 60 participants, subject to readiness and budget.
✓Recommended tracks: climate-smart agribusiness and solar-energy skills.
✓10–12 weeks structured learning followed by transition support.
✓Mentoring and outcome tracking at three, six and twelve months.
